### AgentFold: Closed-Loop Agentic Search for Protein Folding Model Design

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-09-01
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26747
- Matched layers: market_context

We present AgentFold, a multi-agent framework that formulates folding-model development as a closed-loop search over executable code variants. On an engineering-scale protein-folding codebase comprising more than 2,000 lines of code, AgentFold explores approximately 80 model variants using approximately 5,000 GPU-hours and 170 million LLM tokens.

### AcCoRD: Evaluating User-Agent Collaboration Under Realistic User Preference Dynamics

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-09-01
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27818
- Matched layers: market_context

Existing benchmarks for evaluating user-agent collaboration focus almost exclusively on resolving underspecified preferences, thereby failing to capture the richer dynamics of real-world interaction. We introduce AcCoRD, a user-agent collaboration benchmark requiring agents to handle diverse user preference dynamics in two domains: online shopping and travel planning.

### AERA: Adaptive Evidence Residual Allocation for Efficient Test-Time Reasoning

- Published: 2026-09-01
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27964
- Matched layers: ai_compute

Motivated by this mismatch, we introduce Adaptive Evidence Residual Allocation (AERA), a sequential controller that learns whether additional computation is likely to recover a better answer from checkpoint-observable evidence. Across GSM8K and GPQA Diamond, AERA identifies question-specific residual opportunities while substantially reducing inference computation.
