- Published: 2026-08-15
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13472

### AgenticCANN: Automated Ascend C Operator Generation via Knowledge-Augmented Agentic Evolution

- Source: arXiv CS.AI
- Published: 2026-08-15
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26661
- Matched layers: ai_compute

While large language models (LLMs) have shown promise in automated CUDA kernel generation, the fundamentally different programming model of Ascend C introduces unique challenges that remain unexplored. In this paper, we propose AgenticCANN, a knowledge-augmented agentic evolution framework specifically tailored for automated Ascend C operator synthesis in low-corpus NPU environments.
